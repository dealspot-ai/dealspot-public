About DealSpot AI

Revolutionizing small business marketing with artificial intelligence

Our Story

DealSpot AI was born from a simple observation: small businesses struggle with marketing automation. While enterprise companies have entire teams and sophisticated tools, small business owners are juggling multiple roles with limited time and resources.

We set out to change that. Our vision is to democratize AI-powered marketing automation, making enterprise-level capabilities accessible to every small business - from the local restaurant to the independent contractor.

Currently in development, DealSpot AI is building a revolutionary multi-tenant platform that combines social media automation, intelligent voice agents, calendar integration, and powerful workflow automation - all powered by cutting-edge artificial intelligence.

Innovation & Vision

To empower small businesses across all industries - retail, restaurants, trades, construction, healthcare, beauty, automotive, and professional services - with AI-powered tools that level the playing field.

The Platform We're Building

A comprehensive AI ecosystem designed specifically for small business success

Social Media Automation

AI-powered content generation, multi-platform scheduling, and intelligent posting across Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, and more. Our AI learns your brand voice and creates engaging content automatically.

Intelligent Voice Agents

Natural language voice assistants powered by advanced AI models like GPT-4 and ElevenLabs. Handle customer calls, schedule appointments, answer FAQs, and provide personalized support 24/7 in multiple languages.

Calendar Integration

Seamless synchronization with Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar, Calendly, and Acuity. Automated scheduling, smart reminders, and intelligent booking that works across all your platforms.

N8N Workflow Automation

Admin-managed workflow templates that connect your tools and automate complex business processes. No coding required - just select, customize, and deploy powerful automations tailored to your industry.

Multi-Tenant Architecture

Enterprise-grade infrastructure built on Supabase PostgreSQL with row-level security. Your data is completely isolated, encrypted, and secure. GDPR and CCPA compliant from day one.

AI-Powered Analytics

Real-time performance tracking with AI-generated insights. Understand your audience, optimize your campaigns, and make data-driven decisions with recommendations tailored to your business goals.
