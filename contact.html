Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

When will DealSpot AI launch?

We're currently in active development and working hard to launch our mobile and web applications soon. Join our waitlist to be notified when we go live and receive exclusive early access.

What industries do you serve?

DealSpot AI is designed for small businesses across all industries including retail, restaurants, trades, construction, healthcare, beauty, automotive, and professional services. Our platform adapts to your specific industry needs.

How much will it cost?

We're developing tiered subscription plans (Basic, Pro, Enterprise) designed to be affordable for small businesses. Pricing details will be announced closer to launch. Early access members will receive special introductory pricing.

Will there be a free trial?

Yes! We plan to offer a free trial period so you can experience the full power of DealSpot AI before committing to a subscription.

Can I partner with DealSpot AI?

We're open to partnership opportunities! Please use the contact form above and select "Partnership Opportunity" as your subject. We'd love to hear your ideas.
